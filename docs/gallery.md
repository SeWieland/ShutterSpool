# Watch a Gallery slideshow

Use the signed-in owner Gallery to watch photographs without entering Curation.
The slideshow follows the current Gallery filters and Newest first or Oldest first order, including photographs beyond the loaded grid.
Selecting tiles does not choose slideshow membership.

1. In **Gallery → Photos**, choose your filters and order.
2. Choose **Fullscreen photographs** in the Gallery toolbar, then choose **Play**. Opening fullscreen alone does not start playback.
3. Choose **3s**, **5s** or **10s** for each photograph. The default is five seconds, starting when the preview is displayed.
4. Choose **Pause** to stay on a photograph. Previous/Next also pauses playback; **Play** resumes with a full interval.
5. Close fullscreen or press Escape to return to the workspace.

The slideshow loops through current matching photographs.
New arrivals and changed membership can appear as pages are read or on the next loop.
Changing filters or order ends the run; choose Play to use the new query.
Interval choices last only for the current fullscreen visit.

In Gallery and Curation fullscreen, controls hide after three seconds of inactivity.
Move the pointer, tap the photograph or press Tab to bring them back. The first tap wakes hidden controls without activating them.
Keyboard-focused controls and actionable recovery messages stay visible.

## Resume after an interruption

Switching away from the tab or losing the connection pauses playback.
Wait for the workspace to reconnect and refresh, then choose Play when you are ready.
Playback never resumes automatically after an interruption.

If a preview fails or takes longer than 15 seconds, use the viewer's retry action.
After the photograph recovers, choose Play to continue.
A failed preview does not mean the original photograph has been deleted.

If the slideshow cannot load its sequence, choose **Retry** in the viewer.
When it reports that the sequence is available, choose **Play** to continue.

## Viewing does not change your photographs

Playback uses authorized previews, with the full photograph fitted against black.
It does not rate, release or deliver photographs, and it does not extend their availability.
Favorite remains a separate action; read [Favorites and retention](retention.md#favorites-and-other-protections) for its limits.
Removed photographs are no longer available for playback.

The slideshow is for photographs in the owner Gallery.
Curation and Trash do not offer playback, and video playback and shared invitation galleries are not available.
